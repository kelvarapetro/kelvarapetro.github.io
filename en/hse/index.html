Health, safety and environment

Everyone goes home at the end of the rotation the same way they came out.

How we work

Stop work

Everyone on Ødemark A has the right and the duty to stop work they believe is unsafe. No one meets repercussions for using that right.

Risk assessment before work

All work goes through a joint assessment of risk, permits and emergency response before it starts.

Lean crew, clear roles

A small crew means everyone must know their own role and the roles of others. We drill on it regularly.

Emergency preparedness

We drill fire, evacuation and man overboard, and helicopter readiness is part of the plan.

Reporting culture

Report the near misses as well as the incidents. A report takes a few minutes to write, and everyone who sends one gets an answer. Many of the changes we have made on the platform started as an observation from someone on deck.

The crew on Ødemark A has medical care around the clock, and we follow up physical and mental health throughout the rotation.

Whistleblowing: employees and others can report concerns through our independent reporting channel, anonymously if they wish.
