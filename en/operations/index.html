Operations

Ødemark A, the supply base and the fluids plant at Ågotnes.

Ødemark A

The platform

Ødemark A is a Condeep platform. The substructure is concrete, rests on the seabed and is held in place by its own weight. It contains storage cells and three shafts that carry the topside above the sea.

The topside measures 114 × 55 metres and has three levels. The platform is designed for 196 people but runs on a lean crew, a small group in which each person covers several functions.

Ågotnes

Supply base and fluids plant

The base at Ågotnes is Kelvara's supply link to Ødemark A. The supply vessel MS Nordvakt, operated by Aegis Corporation, sails from here, and equipment and consumables are stored here.

Ågotnes Væskeanlegg lies next to the base. The plant mixes and stores drilling fluids and supplies Ødemark A and other operators. It opened in 2014.

Winter operations

Long rotation in winter

From 1 November, Ødemark A moves to its winter schedule. The crew sign on for the whole season until spring, because the weather in the northern Norwegian Sea makes ordinary crew changes and supply runs unreliable.

In the darkest weeks the installation follows Kelvara's Mørketid routines: set times for the daylight lamps, regular contact with family onshore and close follow-up from the medic on board.
