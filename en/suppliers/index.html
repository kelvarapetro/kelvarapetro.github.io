Suppliers

Qualification and invoicing for suppliers to Kelvara.

Qualification

Everyone who delivers goods or services to Ødemark A, the supply base or the fluids plant must be qualified before the first delivery. Qualification covers:

  • Registration in the Register of Business Enterprises and valid tax and VAT certificates
  • HSE self-declaration and an overview of injuries and incidents over the last three years
  • Proof of insurance
  • Statement on due diligence assessments under the Transparency Act

Send qualification enquiries to procurement. We reply within five working days.

innkjop@kelvara.no
