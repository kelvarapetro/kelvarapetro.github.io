About

A company built around one platform

Kelvara was founded in 2011 to buy Ødemark A and get it producing again. We now run the platform, the supply base and the fluids plant at Ågotnes. Since 24 September 2028 all shares in Kelvara have been held by NeuroStat Holding AG.
