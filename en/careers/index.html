Why Kelvara

You join a small crew. You get responsibility early, and the people who have been here a long time show you how things are done. Decisions are made close to the work, and if someone thinks a job is unsafe it stops, whoever ordered it.

How the rotation works

Normally you work two weeks offshore and have four weeks off. From 1 November Ødemark A moves to its winter schedule, and the crew sign on for the whole season until spring. After the season you get a longer stretch off. Supplies and transport go from Ågotnes. Offshore you have your own cabin, three meals a day and a medic available around the clock.

Meet the crew

“Most of my working life has been in the North Sea. Ødemark A is the oldest installation I have run, and she has her habits. The job is to get everybody home at the end of the rotation in the same shape they left. It is a small crew, I know them all by name, and I can usually tell who did not sleep last night. Everyone works real hard here. The best part for me is knowing I did a good job when I see the same faces coming in in the next rotation. Kelvara entrusted me this responsibility, and I am very thankful for that.”
Gunnar MacKenzie
Platform Supervisor, Ødemark A
“I grew up in my family's restaurant, and after that I cooked on a cruise ship and in a hospital kitchen. Out here people work twelve-hour shifts in the cold and the dark, and by mid-afternoon they are already thinking about dinner. Saturday is steak night. I tried changing it once and I will not try that again [Laughter]. Lunch runs a few minutes late now and then. Nobody has complained to my face yet. Except Gunnar.”
Del Carbone
Chief Cook, Ødemark A
“I am the voice on the radio, so everybody ends up talking to me sooner or later. The helicopters and the supply boats check in through me, and so does the crew when something goes wrong down on the cellar deck. I moved around a lot as a kid, and I never expected a platform in the Norwegian Sea to feel like home. It does, though. People here notice when you are not yourself. Living with them in such a small environment really makes you grow fond of strangers.”
Sofie Aune
Radio Operator, Ødemark A
