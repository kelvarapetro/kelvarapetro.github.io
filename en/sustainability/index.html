Sustainability

Emissions, local communities and responsible supply chains.

Emissions

Power on Ødemark A comes from gas turbines, and with such a small crew emissions are low compared with larger installations. In 2027 we emitted 6.9 kilograms of CO₂ per barrel of oil equivalent, against 7.6 kilograms in 2024.

We are continuing work on heat recovery from the turbines and planning supply runs better to use less fuel.

Community and sponsorship

Kelvara is main sponsor of the rescue vessel RS 138 «Sotrabuen», which the Norwegian Society for Sea Rescue runs with a volunteer crew. We also attend careers fairs at upper secondary schools in the region, and we take on apprentices every year.
