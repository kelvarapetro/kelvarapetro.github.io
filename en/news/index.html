    NeuroStat Holding AG acquires Kelvara Petrochemicals AS

    NeuroStat Holding AG (SIX: NEUS) has acquired all shares in Kelvara from Sundvor Partners AS. Kelvara continues as an independent operator.

    Winter operations 2028/29 on Ødemark A

    From 1 November, Ødemark A moves to its winter schedule with a long rotation and Kelvara's routines for the dark season.
