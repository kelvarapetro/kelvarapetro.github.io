Kelvara at the careers fair at Straume vidaregåande skule

Kelvara took part in the careers fair at Straume vidaregåande skule, where students and apprentices spoke with people from Ødemark A and from the base at Ågotnes. Kelvara takes on apprentices in several trades every year.
