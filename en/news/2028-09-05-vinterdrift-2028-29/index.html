Winter operations 2028/29 on Ødemark A

From 1 November, Ødemark A moves to its winter schedule. Crew sign on for the full season until spring, since weather in the northern Norwegian Sea makes regular crew changes and supply runs uncertain over the winter months. Supply runs continue from Ågotnes when the weather allows, and helicopter transfers are limited to medical and operational needs.

Through the darkest weeks the installation follows Kelvara's Mørketid routines, with fixed daylight-lamp schedules, regular contact with families onshore and close follow-up from the installation medic. Families can reach Kelvara's onshore contact team at any hour during the season.
