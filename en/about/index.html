About

Kelvara Petrochemicals AS is a Norwegian oil and gas company headquartered in Stavanger.

Who we are

Kelvara operates Ødemark A in the northern Norwegian Sea and a drilling fluids plant at Ågotnes. About 190 people work for us, offshore and onshore, in small teams where people know each other.

The head office is in Stavanger. The supply base and the fluids plant are at Ågotnes.

History

  • 2011Sundvor Partners AS founds Kelvara to buy and reactivate Ødemark A. The company buys the platform the same year.
  • 2012Production resumes after the topside has been upgraded.
  • 2014Ågotnes Væskeanlegg opens at the supply base.
  • 2027Ødemark A becomes a study site in NeuroStat's research on health and cognitive performance in high-latitude offshore work.
  • 2028Havtil consents to a life extension for Ødemark A through 2038. NeuroStat Holding AG acquires Kelvara on 24 September.

Ownership

Kelvara Petrochemicals AS is owned by Sundvor Partners AS, which founded the company in 2011.

Since 24 September 2028 Kelvara Petrochemicals AS has been wholly owned by NeuroStat Holding AG (SIX: NEUS) in Zürich. From 2011 to 2028 the owner was Sundvor Partners AS.

Kelvara continues as an independent operator under its own name, with the same management and organisation as before.
